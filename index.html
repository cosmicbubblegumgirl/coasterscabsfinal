About us

Built by family. Made for the coast.

Coaster's Cabs

Safe, reliable, comfortable transport

Safe and respectful drivers
Family owned customer care
Reliable local knowledge
Comfortable rides for everyday trips

Born on the South Coast and built by family, our private cab service was created for those moments when you need a lift, but the usual one click options simply are not available.

We are here for the early morning airport runs, the sandy feet after a beach day, the quick trip into town, the safe ride home after dinner, the school run, the shopping mission, the work commute, and every little journey in between. Around here, transport should feel as easy as a sea breeze, not like a struggle.

As a family owned business, we bring a personal touch to every ride. We believe our customers should feel more than just picked up and dropped off. They should feel welcomed, looked after, and completely at ease. Whether you are a local, a holidaymaker, a worker, a student, or someone just trying to get from one coastal stop to the next, we are here to make the trip simple, safe, and comfortable.

Safety is at the heart of what we do. Our service is reliable, respectful, and built on trust. We want every customer to feel calm from the moment they book to the moment they arrive. No stress, no uncertainty, no feeling stranded.

Our cab service was created to fill a real gap along the South Coast, especially in places where transport can be hard to find. We offer a dependable private transport option for the communities, visitors, and families who need someone they can count on.

With a splash of coastal charm, a whole lot of care, and a commitment to honest service, we are proud to help people move around the South Coast safely and comfortably. We are not just a cab service. We are a local family business with big heart, reliable wheels, and a love for keeping our coast connected.
